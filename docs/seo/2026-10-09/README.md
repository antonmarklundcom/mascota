## Data limitations retained

Several supplied meaning groups combine unrelated breeds, brands, establishments or human-health queries. Relevant rows were used as vocabulary; unrelated meanings were excluded from copy. Equal rounded volume/CPC is not proof of synonymy. Do not sum overlapping German shepherd, husky or Persian group totals. The raw main phrase remains in the mapping while copy uses natural Spanish spelling.

San Bernardo's large head query remains ambiguous. The guide covers the dog breed but the reported 42,270 group total is not presented as clean breed demand. American bully, dóberman, pug, salchicha, hurones and pipettes were written as focused general guides using relevant available rows plus authoritative sources; their contaminated totals still require re-export before deciding further investment. The seven groups with fewer than three clean exported variants are flagged in the approved map rather than filled with invented phrases.

The dataset contains 212 project groups but selects only 60 for AI; most selected groups are capped at 50 rows. Selected/capped rows do not prove that a CSV failed. Recover the complete group index and seed-to-file mapping first. Dog vomiting has only 780 searches in exported rows versus the supplied 8,370 full-group total; dog food has 1,780 versus 7,010. Those differences are explicitly retained, not substituted or added.

No brand/competitor landing pages or unverified city pages were created. The paid finder and conditional San Lorenzo page remain later commercial work; Luque/Villarrica intent needs clarification. No free clinic directory or invented businesses are published.

## Editorial sources and medical scope

Sources are listed in `php-site/content/articles.json` and linked visibly beside relevant sections and at the end of each guide. Breed references include AKC, CFA, ABKC, UKC and FCI. Nutrition/product references include WSAVA, FDA and RSPCA. Symptom guides use MSD, Cornell and ASPCA. Existing Paraguay guides retain official institutional sources.

The four new symptom guides explain observations, consultation preparation and urgent signs. They do not diagnose, give medicine doses, recommend human remedies or claim professional review. The hurón guide separates general husbandry from Paraguay acquisition/tenure/import checks with official authorities. A source consulted date is distinct from professional review. Nutrition examples are labelled calculations, not current retail prices.
